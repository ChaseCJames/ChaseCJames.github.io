03 · ethics

Disclose by default. Be skeptical by default.

Disclosure-by-default and skepticism-by-default. When in doubt about whether to disclose, disclose. When in doubt about whether a hosted model can be trusted with a piece of data, assume it cannot.

Before sending — five questions
  1. 01Who created this data, and what did they expect would happen to it?
  2. 02What does the hosted model's terms of service say about retention and training?
  3. 03Could the data be reconstructed from the model's output, weeks or months later?
  4. 04If this data leaked, what would it cost — reputationally, legally, to its subjects?
  5. 05Is there a way to do this without sending the data itself?
