What this looks like in practice.

A real working group's setup, after about a year of figuring it out. The structure should transfer; the specific tools reflect this lab's people, science, and resources. Expect your own version to look similar in shape and different in detail.

Work in progress what to improve next

The structure carries the weight; the rough edges below are real and worth naming.

Heavy for small tasks
A one-off figure regeneration does not really need a planning summary, a script header, and a pre-flight gate. The boundary is fuzzy.
Cross-project memory
Lessons learned in one project do not naturally propagate. The framework needs a better story for lab-wide memory.
Onboarding cost
A new member needs roughly 2–3 weeks to operate fluently. The right cost, but a cost.
Tools change fast
CLI-entry-point files get rewritten as the underlying tooling evolves. Maintenance is ongoing.
