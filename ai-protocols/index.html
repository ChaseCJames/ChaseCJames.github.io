A framework for
AI tools in science.

01Put hypothesis-driven science back at the center.
02Less plumbing, more thinking.
03Reproducibility before throughput.

AI tools let scientists streamline tasks that don't need to be hands-on — efficiently prepping model runs for HPC, generating figures during research and for publication, the kind of work that takes time but can be managed rather than done directly. This frees scientists up to focus on the parts that always mattered most: choosing the question, designing the test, reading the data, deciding what it means. This framework is for doing that deliberately, not by accident.

Why a separate framework for science
01
Reproducibility
A result another scientist cannot regenerate is not a result.
02
Falsifiability
Agents can produce plausible-sounding conclusions; we need structure designed to defend against this.
03
Credit
Authorship is a moral claim about who did the work. When agents contribute, the lab needs an explicit way to document the human contribution.
